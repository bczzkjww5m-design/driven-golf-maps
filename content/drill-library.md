# Driven Golf — Practice Drill Library (v1)

*Written October 11, 2026. Source content for the app's practice prescriptions. Every recommendation the app makes must point to a drill here, so the golfer is told how to practice, not just what to practice.*

## How every prescription is built

Each prescription has six parts, always in this order:

1. **Why (your data):** the stat that triggered it, in one sentence, for example "Your first putts from 20–40 ft finish 6 ft away on average."
2. **The drill:** name and time needed.
3. **Setup:** what to lay out, in numbered steps.
4. **How to do it:** numbered steps, plus **one** feel or technique cue (never a list of swing thoughts).
5. **Score and goal:** how to keep score in the app and what counts as passing.
6. **Make it harder:** a pressure version, and when to move to it (pass the goal in 2 sessions in a row).

Then a **course check**: the stat the app will watch in the next rounds to see whether the practice transferred.

## Practice principles the app follows

- **Score everything.** Every drill has a number, so practice can be compared with the course.
- **Mix it up.** After the first few reps, change club, distance or lie on every ball. Random practice transfers to the course better than hitting the same shot over and over.
- **One ball, full routine** for scoring reps, like on the course.
- **Add consequences.** Pressure versions have a "start over" or "beat your score" rule.
- **Small doses.** Prescriptions fit 15, 30 or 60 minutes.
- **Retest on the course.** The app compares the drill's stat before and after over the next 3 rounds.

## Triggers: which stat leads to which drill

| Weakness (from round or practice data) | Trigger (suggested) | Drills |
|---|---|---|
| Lag putting | First-putt leave > 4 ft from 20+ ft, or 3-putts > 10% of holes | Lag Ladder |
| Short putts | Make rate from 3–6 ft below 80% | Clock Drill, Start-Line Gate |
| Chips from the rough | Up-and-down from rough lies below 50%, or rough chips finishing > 8 ft | Rough Chip Circuit |
| Chip distance control | Chips from fringe or fairway finishing > 6 ft on average | Landing Spot Towel |
| Overall short game / scrambling | Scrambling below 50% | Up-and-Down Circuit (9-spot or 30-minute) |
| Bunker play | Bunker shots leaving the ball in the bunker, or finishing > 15 ft | Bunker Line Drill |
| Wedge distances (40–110 yd) | Wedge approaches finishing > 20 ft on average, or mostly short/long | Wedge Ladder |
| Approach accuracy | Greens in regulation below target, or misses mostly one side | Nine-Ball Target Game |
| Tee shot accuracy | Fairways or "in play" below 60%, or misses mostly one side | Fairway Corridor |
| Course management | Plan-vs-actual shows repeated misses to the danger side | Play the Course on the Range |

## The drills

### Lag Ladder (putting, 15 min)
- **Setup:** 1) Pick a hole with some slope. 2) Put tees at 20, 30 and 40 ft. 3) Lay a club or string a putter-length (about 3 ft) around the hole as the "safe zone."
- **How:** 1) Hit one ball from each distance, rotating 20 → 40 → 30, nine balls in all. 2) Full routine every putt: read, two practice strokes looking at the hole, then go. 3) Hole out the second putt every time.
- **Cue:** "Rehearse the stroke while looking at the hole" — your eyes set the distance, not your hands.
- **Score:** 2 points if holed, 1 inside the safe zone, 0 outside. Goal: 10 of 18 points.
- **Harder:** Shrink the zone to 2 ft, or require all three 40-footers inside the zone before you can stop.
- **Course check:** average leave on first putts over 20 ft, and 3-putts per round.

### Clock Drill (short putts, 10–15 min)
- **Setup:** 4 tees around the hole at 3 ft (12, 3, 6 and 9 o'clock), on a slope if possible. Then rings at 4, 5 and 6 ft.
- **How:** 1) Go around the 3-ft ring. 2) Make all four to move out to the next ring. 3) Miss one and drop back a ring.
- **Cue:** "Pick a spot 2 inches in front of the ball on your line and roll it over the spot."
- **Score:** the farthest ring completed. Goal: complete the 5-ft ring.
- **Harder:** a miss sends you back to 3 ft, and you must complete the 6-ft ring.
- **Course check:** make rate from 3–6 ft.

### Start-Line Gate (short putts, 10 min)
- **Setup:** a straight 6-ft putt; two tees 12 inches in front of the ball, just wider than a ball apart.
- **How:** 10 putts through the gate, full routine each time.
- **Cue:** "Square face, smooth tempo — the gate only cares about start line."
- **Score:** putts through the gate and holed. Goal: 8 of 10.
- **Harder:** narrow the gate, or move it to 8 ft.
- **Course check:** make rate from 3–10 ft.

### Rough Chip Circuit (chipping, 20 min)
- **Setup:** 3 balls in different rough lies around one green: sitting up, sitting down, and growing against you. Use 3 different pins.
- **How:** 1) Read the lie first: sitting down = more loft and a steeper strike, sitting up = careful not to slide under it. 2) Pick a landing spot and club (58° or a lower-lofted club to run it). 3) One ball, then putt out. 4) Rotate lies and pins every ball, nine shots in all.
- **Cue:** "Lead arm low and wide on the takeaway, hands lead through impact." (This fixed chunked chips on Oct 7.)
- **Score:** up-and-downs out of 9. Goal: 6 of 9.
- **Harder:** two worst lies only, and you must beat your last score.
- **Course check:** up-and-down rate from rough lies.

### Landing Spot Towel (chip distance control, 15 min)
- **Setup:** a small towel on the green 3–5 ft onto the putting surface; a hole 20–40 ft away.
- **How:** 1) Chip with three clubs (58°, PW, 8-iron), changing club every ball. 2) Land each one on the towel and watch how far each club releases. 3) Then putt out the best ball of each set.
- **Cue:** "Same small stroke, different club — let the loft do the work."
- **Score:** balls landing on the towel out of 15. Goal: 9 of 15.
- **Harder:** move the towel each set and call the club before seeing the lie.
- **Course check:** average finish distance on chips from the fringe and fairway.

### Up-and-Down Circuit (short game under pressure, 30–60 min)
- **Setup:** 9 spots around a practice green, all different: fringe, rough, tight lie, uphill, downhill, short-sided, a long pitch, a bunker and one flop. 30-minute version: 6 spots.
- **How:** 1) One ball per spot, play it like the course: read, pick a club, routine, then putt out. 2) Don't re-hit. 3) Log each spot in the app.
- **Cue:** "Pick the shot with the biggest margin for error, not the prettiest one."
- **Score:** up-and-downs out of 9 (or 6), chip-ins count double. Goal: 5 of 9 (3 of 6). Aaron's Oct 7 score: 7 of 9 with 1 chip-in.
- **Harder:** you must beat your last total, or restart the circuit if you fail two in a row.
- **Course check:** scrambling percentage.

### Bunker Line Drill (bunker play, 15 min)
- **Setup:** draw a line in the sand perpendicular to your target, then a second line 2 inches behind it.
- **How:** 1) Without a ball, enter the sand on the back line 10 times so the divot starts there. 2) Put a ball on the front line and repeat 10 times. 3) Then 10 balls to a pin, changing lies.
- **Cue:** "Splash the sand onto the green — the ball rides out on it."
- **Score:** balls on the green out of 10, then up-and-downs from 5 balls. Goal: 8 of 10 on the green, 2 of 5 up-and-down.
- **Harder:** plugged and downhill lies only.
- **Course check:** sand saves and bunker shots that stay in the bunker.

### Wedge Ladder (40–110 yd, 20 min)
- **Setup:** flags or targets at 50, 60, 70, 80, 90 and 100 yd (a launch monitor helps; a rangefinder works).
- **How:** 1) Hit 12 balls, calling a random distance each time (the app can call them). 2) Choose the club and swing (½, ¾, full) from My Bag. 3) Log each shot's distance.
- **Cue:** "Same tempo every swing — change the length of the backswing, not the speed."
- **Score:** shots within 5 yd of the called distance. Goal: 8 of 12.
- **Harder:** within 3 yd, or add 45 and 105 yd.
- **Course check:** average proximity on approaches from 40–110 yd. Logged shots also update the partial-swing distances in My Bag (with the golfer's OK).

### Nine-Ball Target Game (approach accuracy, 20 min)
- **Setup:** pick a green-sized target (about 30 yd wide) at 140–180 yd.
- **How:** 1) Hit 9 balls, changing club and target every ball: 3 short irons, 3 mid irons, 3 hybrids or long irons. 2) Full routine. 3) Note where each miss went: short, long, left or right.
- **Cue:** "Pick a small target inside the big one, and commit before you swing."
- **Score:** balls finishing on the "green" out of 9. Goal: 5 of 9. The app shows the miss pattern.
- **Harder:** play to the side of the green your plan says is safe, and only count balls on that side.
- **Course check:** greens in regulation and the share of misses on the planned side.

### Fairway Corridor (tee shots, 15 min)
- **Setup:** pick two targets on the range about 30 yd apart as the edges of a fairway.
- **How:** 1) Hit 10 tee shots, alternating driver and 3 wood (or your tee clubs). 2) Full routine every ball, including picking a target. 3) Optional: play the first 9 tee shots of your home course from your plan, in order.
- **Cue:** "Tee it the same height, same ball position, and finish in balance."
- **Score:** balls finishing in the corridor out of 10. Goal: 7 of 10.
- **Harder:** narrow the corridor to 25 yd, or play your course's tightest holes.
- **Course check:** fairways hit and tee shots in play.

### Play the Course on the Range (management, 30 min)
- **Setup:** open your plan for your next round.
- **How:** 1) For each hole, hit the planned tee club to the planned target, then the planned approach club to a green-sized target. 2) Full routine every ball, never the same club twice in a row. 3) Score each hole: target hit = par, miss to the safe side = bogey, miss to the danger side = double.
- **Cue:** "Commit to the plan — the shot's job is the target, not the pin."
- **Score:** your "range round" score for 9 holes. Goal: within 3 of par.
- **Harder:** play all 18, or use the Aggressive plan.
- **Course check:** plan-vs-actual: shots finishing within 15 yd of target and misses to the danger side.

## Building a session

The app combines drills to fit the time available, weakest area first:
- **15 min:** one drill.
- **30 min:** the top drill plus one short-putt drill (Clock or Gate) to finish under pressure.
- **60 min:** two drills for the top weakness and one for the second, then finish with 9 balls of the Up-and-Down Circuit.
